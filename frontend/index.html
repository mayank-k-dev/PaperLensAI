Biostatistical Rigor & Grounding: Enforces explicit extraction of Hazard Ratios, 95% Confidence Intervals, and p-values directly from study text.

Hallucination Defense: Constrains generation strictly to reported findings; unmentioned metrics are flagged rather than fabricated.
